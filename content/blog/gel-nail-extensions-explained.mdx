A gel nail extension adds real length using a gel tip or sculpted gel form over your natural nail — no acrylic monomer at any point. Extensions have a reputation for meaning acrylic; ours don't. Here's what to expect from one.

## What it is

A **Gel Nail Extension** adds real length using a gel tip or sculpted gel form over your natural nail, then finishes with the same gel-polish top coat as every other service on our menu — no acrylic monomer involved at any point. It's the right call if your natural nail is short, uneven, or prone to breaking and you want a specific length and shape to work with, rather than building around whatever's already there.

## Realistic shapes and lengths

We shape to your natural nail bed rather than pushing toward the most dramatic length possible — a moderate coffin, square, or almond shape holds up far better day-to-day than an extreme length, and it's still a real, noticeable length change from a bare or damaged nail. If you have a specific shape in mind, mention it when you book; it changes how much product and shaping time your appointment needs.

## Upkeep

Gel extensions need more frequent attention than a plain overlay, since your natural nail keeps growing underneath the shaped tip:

- Expect a fill appointment every 2–3 weeks as regrowth becomes visible at the cuticle line.
- Between visits, treat them like healthy nails, not armor — cuticle oil daily, gloves for anything abrasive.
- When you're ready to stop, ask for a proper soak-off removal rather than picking or prying — see our [gel removal guide](/blog/safe-gel-removal-explained) for what that actually involves.

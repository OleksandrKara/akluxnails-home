Our pedicure menu is deliberately short — two real options, not a long list of near-duplicates. Here's the actual difference.

## Regular Pedicure Gel-Overlay (Dry)

A full pedicure — soak, shaping, cuticle work, callus care — finished with a gel-polish overlay for a glossy, chip-resistant color that holds up for weeks, the same gel-only approach as every manicure on our menu (see [why we don't use acrylic](/blog/no-acrylics-nail-health), same reasoning applies here). "Dry" describes our cuticle-care approach, not a lack of a soak — you still get the full treatment, just without the tools that lead to a hurried job.

![Soft pink gel pedicure](/images/blog/pedicure-soft-pink.jpg)

## Pedicure (No Polish)

The same full treatment — soak, shaping, cuticle and callus care — with nothing on top. This is the one to book if you want the hygiene and precision of a real pedicure without committing to a color, or if you're planning to apply your own polish afterward.

![Deep burgundy gel pedicure](/images/blog/pedicure-burgundy.jpg)

## How to pick

| Your situation | Book this |
|---|---|
| Want color that lasts weeks without chipping | Regular Pedicure Gel-Overlay (Dry) |
| Want the treatment with no color, or plan to polish yourself | Pedicure (No Polish) |

Men's pedicures follow the same no-polish format — see our [men's services guide](/blog/mens-manicure-and-4-hand-appointments) for details.

Both pedicure options are backed by the same [2-week guarantee](/blog/2-week-guarantee-explained) as every manicure on our menu.

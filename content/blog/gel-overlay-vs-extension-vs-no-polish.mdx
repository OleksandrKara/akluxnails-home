The service menu names sound similar, but they solve different problems. Here's the actual difference between the three manicures people ask about most.

## Gel-Overlay Manicure — for your natural nail, protected

This is a layer of hard gel applied directly over your own nail — no added length, no tips. It's the closest thing to "no manicure at all" in terms of look, but with a protective gel layer and the dry-cuticle prep described in our [Russian manicure explainer](/blog/russian-manicure-explained). Book this if your natural nails are a length and shape you're happy with and you want them protected, glossy, and finished — not extended.

## Gel Nail Extension — for added length

This adds real length using a gel tip or sculpted gel form, then finishes with the same gel-polish top coat. It's the right call if your natural nail is short, uneven, or prone to breaking and you want a specific length and shape rather than working with what's already there. Extensions need slightly more maintenance as your natural nail grows underneath — expect to come back for a fill sooner than a pure overlay.

## No-Polish Manicure — for the health-focused, no-color option

Full shaping, cuticle work, and buffing, with a clear finish or none at all — no gel, no polish. This is the one to book if you want the hygiene and precision of a real manicure without any coating on top: before a wedding where you'll be reapplying your own polish, between gel sets to let your nails breathe, or simply if you don't wear color. If you want a treatment built specifically around strengthening the nail itself rather than just skipping polish, see our [Japanese manicure explainer](/blog/japanese-manicure-explained) too.

## European Express Manicure — for a quicker visit

A shorter-format version of the gel-overlay service, for when you want the same gel-polish finish but have less time on the clock. Ask us at booking whether it fits what you're looking for that day — it's the right trade-off for a quick touch-up, not a substitute for the full treatment if your nails need more attention.

## Quick decision guide

| Your situation | Book this |
|---|---|
| Nails are a length/shape you like, want them protected + glossy | Gel-Overlay Manicure |
| Nails are short or uneven, want specific length | Gel Nail Extension |
| Want a clean, healthy manicure with no color | No-Polish Manicure |
| Want the fastest, lower-key version of the overlay | European Express Manicure |

Whichever you pick, none of them involve acrylic — see [why we don't offer it](/blog/no-acrylics-nail-health) if you're curious about that decision.

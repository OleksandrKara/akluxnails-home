If you've searched for extra-long or "size X"-style extensions specifically, here's the direct answer: we don't offer dramatic, maximum-length extensions as a distinct option, and it's worth explaining why rather than just saying no.

## What limits realistic length

A [Gel Nail Extension](/blog/gel-nail-extensions-explained) is only as strong as the bond between the extension and your natural nail bed. The longer the extension, the more leverage everyday impact has on that bond — typing, opening a car door, anything that catches the tip. Past a certain point, added length stops being a styling choice and starts being a structural weak point, regardless of how well it's applied.

Acrylic can push further into extreme length because it's rigid enough to resist that leverage, at the cost of the flexibility trade-offs covered in our [acrylic vs. gel breakdown](/blog/no-acrylics-nail-health). Since we don't offer acrylic at all, we shape every extension to a length your natural nail bed can genuinely support long-term.

## What we shape to instead

A moderate coffin, square, or almond length — long enough to be a real, noticeable change from a bare or damaged nail, short enough to survive normal daily use without your gel bond becoming the first thing to fail. If you have a specific length in mind, mention it at booking; we'll tell you honestly whether it's realistic for your natural nail or where it tends to hold up better a size or two shorter.

## The trade-off, plainly

If maximum length is the priority over durability, that's a real preference — it's just not one we've built this menu around. Our [full extensions guide](/blog/gel-nail-extensions-explained) covers the shapes and lengths we do work with, and how fill maintenance works once you've picked one.

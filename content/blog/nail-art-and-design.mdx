"Nail design" covers a huge range in the industry — anything from a single accent nail to hand-painted 3D work. Here's specifically what that means when you book with us, with real examples from real appointments.

## It's an add-on, not a separate service

Nail art at AK.LUX.NAILS is booked as an add-on to a manicure — [Russian Gel-Overlay](/blog/russian-manicure-explained), [Gel Nail Extension](/blog/gel-overlay-vs-extension-vs-no-polish), or any of our other gel services — not a standalone appointment. When you book, you'll see two design options:

- **Design** — a defined pattern, accent detail, or hand-painted element on one or more nails.
- **Ombre (design)** — a gradient/blended color effect, either on its own or as the base for further detail.

Only one of these applies per set (they're grouped as a single choice at booking), so the simplest way to get exactly what you want is to describe the look when you book, or bring a reference photo to your appointment — a minimalist single dot pattern and a full hand-painted floral set both fall under "Design," but they take very different amounts of chair time.

## Getting the timing right

Because design work is priced and timed as an add-on, tell us what you're picturing when you book (not after you're already in the chair) — it's the difference between an appointment that runs on schedule and one where a same-day design change pushes into someone else's booking. If you're not sure exactly what you want yet, a rough description ("simple accent nail," "something seasonal," "hand-painted") is enough for us to plan the right amount of time.

Every gel set — with or without design — is backed by the same [2-week guarantee](/blog/2-week-guarantee-explained): if something about the finish isn't right, we fix it free.

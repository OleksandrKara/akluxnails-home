If you're looking for a manicure in San Diego and trying to figure out where to book, here's the short version of what we actually do — no padded lists of services we don't offer, just what's on the real menu at our Downtown studio.

## The core idea: gel-only, technique-first

Every manicure and pedicure here uses hard gel and gel polish — we don't offer acrylic at all, on any service (see [why we made that call](/blog/no-acrylics-nail-health)). Cuticle work is done with the dry, e-file [Russian manicure technique](/blog/russian-manicure-explained), which is why our gel tends to outlast a standard set. Every service is backed by the same [2-week guarantee](/blog/2-week-guarantee-explained): if something's not right within 14 days, we fix it free.

## What's actually on the menu

- **[Russian Gel-Overlay Manicure](/blog/russian-manicure-explained)** — gel over your natural nail, no added length.
- **[Gel Nail Extension](/blog/gel-nail-extensions-explained)** — real added length using a gel tip or sculpted form.
- **No-Polish Manicure** — full shaping and cuticle care, no gel or color. See our [full comparison of these three](/blog/gel-overlay-vs-extension-vs-no-polish) if you're not sure which fits.
- **European Express Manicure** — a shorter-format gel-overlay for tighter schedules.
- **[Japanese manicure](/blog/japanese-manicure-explained)** — a treatment built around strengthening the natural nail itself.
- **Pedicures** — the same [gel-overlay or no-polish choice](/blog/pedicure-options-explained) as our manicures.
- **Men's manicures and pedicures** — the same care, finished without polish. See our [men's services guide](/blog/mens-manicure-and-4-hand-appointments).
- **Nail art** — a [design or ombre add-on](/blog/nail-art-and-design) to any of the above, not a standalone booking.

## Currently wearing acrylic, or coming off gel?

Two guides specifically for that: [switching from acrylic to gel](/blog/switching-from-acrylic-to-gel) if you're moving over, or [safe gel removal](/blog/safe-gel-removal-explained) if you just need a clean soak-off, whether or not you're rebooking with us the same day.

## Where we are

We're a small studio in Downtown San Diego — one of the reasons cuticle work here is done at a deliberate pace rather than rushed through a high-volume schedule.

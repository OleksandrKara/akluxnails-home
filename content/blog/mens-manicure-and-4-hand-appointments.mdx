Two questions come up often enough to be worth a straight answer: what's actually different about a men's manicure, and what is a "4-hand" appointment?

## Men's manicures and pedicures — same care, no polish

Our **Men's Regular Manicure** and **Men's Regular Pedicure** are the same shaping, cuticle care, and buffing as the rest of our menu — hygienic tools, the same attention to detail — just finished without polish, which is what most of our male clients are actually looking for: well-groomed, healthy-looking nails without a color finish.

That said, "men's" describes the finish, not a restriction — if you'd rather have a gel overlay, a specific color, or any other service on our menu, that's open to anyone, no separate booking category required. Book whichever service actually matches what you want.

## 4-Hand appointments — both hands and feet, at once

A 4-hand appointment means two of our technicians work on you simultaneously — one on your hands, one on your feet — so a full manicure-and-pedicure visit takes roughly half the chair time of doing them back-to-back.

Because it needs two technicians scheduled and available at the same time, this isn't an instantly-confirmed online booking like our other services — request it and we'll follow up directly to lock in a time that works for both artists' schedules. Pricing is worked out on that follow-up call based on which services you want for hands and feet; as a starting reference point, combined 4-hand appointments typically begin around **$249**.

It's the right call if your schedule is tight and you want the full experience without the full time commitment — otherwise, either service works perfectly well booked on its own.

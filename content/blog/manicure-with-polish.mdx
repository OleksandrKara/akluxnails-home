## What actually separates the two

Regular polish air-dries in layers and is fully removable with acetone-free remover in a couple of minutes. Gel polish is a similar-looking lacquer that only hardens under UV/LED light, which is the entire reason it holds up for weeks instead of days:

| | Regular polish | Gel polish |
|---|---|---|
| Cures by | Air-drying | UV/LED light |
| Typical wear before chipping | 2–5 days | 2–3 weeks or more |
| Removal | Any polish remover | Soak-off (see [our removal guide](/blog/safe-gel-removal-explained)) |
| Touch-up flexibility | Easy to redo yourself same-day | Committed until your next appointment |

## Why we standardized on gel

A manicure's finish is only as good as how long it actually lasts, and regular polish chips well before most people are ready to redo it — which is the main complaint it gets. Gel closes that gap at the cost of a small trade-off: you're committed to the color until your next visit or a proper removal, rather than able to swap it same-day with your own bottle at home.

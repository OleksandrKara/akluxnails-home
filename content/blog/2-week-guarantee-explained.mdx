Our guarantee is simple: if there is a workmanship or product issue with your set within 14 days of your appointment, we fix it free. Booking a manicure sight-unseen is a real hesitation — what if the color's off, or a nail lifts a week later? Here's exactly how that works, in plain terms.

## What's covered

If an issue caused by our work, products or technique shows up within **14 days of your appointment** — lifting, peeling, early chipping at the edges, a shaping issue. We'll fix it, free. You don't need to prove fault; just let us know within the window.

## What's not covered

The guarantee does not cover natural wear, or damage caused by the client: scratching or rubbing a nail, catching it on something, a nail breaking, or normal growth-out. Nails are in constant daily use, so some wear is expected.

## How to actually use it

1. **Notify us within 48 hours of noticing the issue** (and always within 14 days of your appointment).
2. Reach out by phone or text — the same number you'd use to book.
3. We'll get you in for a complimentary fix appointment.

Also not covered is a completed service you simply changed your mind about after the fact — services are non-refundable once done, and results can vary a little based on personal preference, which is exactly why we'd rather fix a real issue than argue about it. If you want something adjusted, tell us during the appointment itself; that's always free and easy to accommodate on the spot.

## Why we can offer this at all

A 2-week guarantee only makes sense if chips and lifting are actually rare — otherwise it'd be a constant cost to the business, not a real promise. That's a direct consequence of two things covered elsewhere on this blog: the [dry-cuticle Russian manicure technique](/blog/russian-manicure-explained), which is the single biggest factor in how well gel bonds to the nail, and [never using acrylic](/blog/no-acrylics-nail-health), which means nothing on your nail is fighting against natural flex and popping loose. The guarantee isn't a separate perk bolted on top — it's what the process is confident enough to back up.

## Booking and cancellation, briefly

Since this comes up alongside the guarantee: appointments ask for a card on file, which is only ever charged for a no-show or a cancellation inside 24 hours ($25) — never for the service itself. Reschedule or cancel with more notice than that and there's no charge at all.

Our guarantee is simple: if there is a workmanship or product issue with your set within 14 days of your appointment, we fix it free. Booking a manicure sight-unseen is a real hesitation, so here are the terms in plain language, including what we do not cover and why.

## What's covered

Anything caused by our work, our products or our technique, if it shows up within **14 days of your appointment**. You don't need to prove fault, just let us know. For example:

- Lifting or peeling of the gel, especially near the cuticle or the sidewalls
- Early chipping or flaking at the edges that is not caused by an impact
- Bubbles, cracks or uneven spots that start in the product itself
- A shape, length or color that is not what we agreed on during your appointment

## What's not covered

The guarantee does not cover natural wear or damage caused by the client. Nails are in constant daily use, so some wear is normal and expected with any manicure.

**Natural wear** is the gradual change that comes from living with your hands:

- Gradual loss of shine, or light surface scratches that build up over the weeks
- Small wear or tiny chips at the free edge of the nail from everyday tasks
- The visible gap at the cuticle as your natural nail grows out (normal growth-out)

**Damage caused by the client** is anything that happens from a specific impact, habit or exposure:

- A nail that breaks or cracks from catching it on fabric, hair, a zipper or a bag
- Scratching, rubbing or picking at the gel, or peeling it off
- Using nails as tools (opening cans, scraping labels, pressing buttons)
- Hitting or slamming a finger, or a piece breaking off from impact
- Prolonged contact with harsh chemicals or very hot water without gloves

If you are not sure which side an issue falls on, message us a photo and we will tell you honestly. Real workmanship issues are always fixed, and anything else we are happy to repair at our regular price.

Also not covered is a completed service you simply changed your mind about after the fact. Services are non-refundable once done, and results can vary a little based on personal preference. If you want something adjusted, tell us during the appointment itself, that is always free and easy to accommodate on the spot.

## How to use it

1. **Notify us within 48 hours of noticing the issue**, and always within 14 days of your appointment.
2. Reach out by phone or text, the same number you'd use to book. A photo helps.
3. We'll get you in for a complimentary fix appointment.

## Why we can offer this at all

A 2-week guarantee only makes sense if lifting and early chipping are actually rare, otherwise it'd be a constant cost to the business, not a real promise. That's a direct consequence of two things covered elsewhere on this blog: the [dry-cuticle Russian manicure technique](/blog/russian-manicure-explained), which is the single biggest factor in how well gel bonds to the nail, and [never using acrylic](/blog/no-acrylics-nail-health), which means nothing on your nail is fighting against natural flex and popping loose. The guarantee isn't a separate perk bolted on top, it's what the process is confident enough to back up.

## Booking and cancellation, briefly

Since this comes up alongside the guarantee: appointments ask for a card on file, which is only ever charged for a no-show or a cancellation inside 24 hours ($25), never for the service itself. Reschedule or cancel with more notice than that and there's no charge at all.
